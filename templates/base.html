✅ BookWorm is installed! You already have an AI Search shortcut.

  2. 2. Tap ✨ AI Search from the shortcuts menu
  3. 3. The AI search panel opens instantly — no navigation needed!

  1. 1. Tap the Share button in Safari (box with arrow ↑)
  2. 2. Choose Add to Home Screen
  3. 3. The shortcut will open straight into AI Search
